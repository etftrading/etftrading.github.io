Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

We collect as little personal information as possible. You can read this website without creating an account or telling us who you are.

Information we collect

  • Usage data — pages viewed, device and browser type, approximate location and referring site, collected through privacy-respecting analytics.
  • Information you send us — your email address and message when you contact us.
  • Calculator inputs — processed in your browser only; we do not store them.

Cookies and partner tracking

When you click a partner link, the partner may place cookies or use similar technologies to record that you came from our site. This lets them pay us a commission. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

How we use information

To operate and improve the site, understand which content is useful, respond to messages, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including California under the CCPA/CPRA — you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete personal information and to opt out of certain sharing. To make a request, contact us at privacy@insert_domain.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.
