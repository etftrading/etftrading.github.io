How Leveraged ETFs Work

They use financial derivatives (swaps and futures) to amplify an index's daily return, rebalancing daily. Because of daily compounding, they are designed primarily for active intraday and short-term traders.

  • Index up 10%, then down 10%: the index is down 1% — a 3× fund is down 9%.
  • In steady trends, leverage can beat 3×; in choppy markets it loses ground.
  • They can lose most of their value quickly — single-stock funds even within one day.

Day Trading Leveraged ETFs: The 2026 Rule Change

FINRA's $25,000 pattern day trader rule was replaced by new intraday margin standards, effective June 4, 2026. Brokers may phase in the change until October 20, 2027, so check your broker's current rules.

  • A $2,000 minimum still applies to margin accounts.
  • You don't need margin to buy leveraged ETFs — they can be bought in a cash account.

Leveraged ETF Outside the US

UK and EU retail investors can't buy US leveraged ETFs such as TQQQ or UPRO, which lack a PRIIPs Key Information Document. Europe has its own 3× leveraged ETPs, such as WisdomTree QQQ3, but brokers may restrict them as complex products; Australians use Betashares geared funds such as GGUS.

Leveraged ETF FAQ

Can you hold TQQQ long term?

TQQQ is designed for short holding periods. Because leverage resets daily, long-term compounding diverges from 3× the index — delivering outsized gains in sustained uptrends, but compounding losses during choppy or declining markets.

Can a leveraged ETF go to zero?

A 3× fund would experience severe capital loss in a single-day index drop approaching 33%, and single-stock 2× or 3× funds move even faster. Most exchanges feature circuit breakers to halt trading during extreme market drops.

What is volatility decay?

The loss leveraged funds suffer when prices swing up and down, because daily percentage changes compound unevenly. Try it in the calculator above.

Are leveraged ETFs good for beginners?

Leveraged ETFs are specialized tactical instruments. They require active monitoring and disciplined risk management, making plain index ETFs like VOO or QQQ much more suitable for long-term core portfolios.

Do I need $25,000 to day trade leveraged ETFs?

Not anymore under FINRA's new rules, effective June 4, 2026 — but brokers can phase the change in until October 2027, so some may still apply the old limit.

Can UK investors buy TQQQ?

Generally no — TQQQ lacks the Key Information Document UK rules require, and leveraged products are treated as complex. Some UK brokers offer European equivalents such as QQQ3 after an appropriateness check.
