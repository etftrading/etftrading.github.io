Short-Term vs. Long-Term Treasury ETFs

T-bill ETFs barely move in price. Long-term funds pay more but carry rate risk: TLT, with a duration of 14.6 years, would lose about 14.6% of its value if long-term rates rose one percentage point.

  • Parking cash for months: SGOV, BIL or SHV.
  • Betting on falling rates: IEF or TLT — prices rise when rates fall.

The State-Tax Advantage of Treasury ETFs

Interest from US Treasuries is exempt from state and local income tax under 31 U.S.C. §3124. For ETFs, the exempt share is reported each year — 95.14% for SGOV and 99.64% for TLT in 2025.

  • Some states, such as California, Connecticut and New York, require a fund to hold at least 50% in federal obligations.
  • The interest is still subject to federal income tax.
  • For stock-market income, compare dividend ETFs.

Treasury ETF Outside the US

UK and EU retail investors use Ireland-domiciled UCITS Treasury ETFs such as IB01 or IDTL, because US-listed Treasury ETFs lack a PRIIPs Key Information Document; UK savers can also hold gilts through IGLS. Australians can use the AUD-hedged IUSG, or AAA as a cash-rate alternative.

Treasury ETF FAQ

Is SGOV better than a savings account?

SGOV yielded 3.71% (Oct 7, 2026) versus a 0.37% national average savings rate, and its income is largely state-tax free. Top high-yield savings accounts pay about 4.2–4.3% and are FDIC insured, so compare after tax with the calculator.

Is SGOV safe?

SGOV holds ultra-short US Treasury bills backed by the full faith and credit of the US government. While not bank deposits, Treasuries carry top-tier sovereign credit quality, and SGOV's share price remains exceptionally stable.

What is the difference between SGOV and BIL?

Both hold short-term T-bills. SGOV holds 0–3 month bills and charges 0.09%; BIL holds 1–3 month bills and charges 0.1353%.

Why did TLT lose so much value?

TLT holds 20+ year bonds, whose prices fall sharply when long-term interest rates rise. Its duration of about 14.6 years makes it very rate-sensitive.

How often do Treasury ETFs pay?

Most pay monthly, including SGOV, BIL, SHV, IEF and TLT.

Can UK investors buy US Treasury ETFs?

Not the US-listed versions in most retail accounts. UCITS Treasury ETFs such as IB01 or IDTL hold the same US government bonds and are available on the London Stock Exchange.
