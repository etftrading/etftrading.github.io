Bitcoin ETF or Real Bitcoin?

Choose an ETF for IRAs and simplicity. Choose bitcoin itself if you want to hold your own keys, trade 24/7, send BTC or avoid a yearly fee.

  • ETF: trades only during market hours, no wallet or keys, works in retirement accounts.
  • Direct: buy on an exchange such as Kraken, withdraw to your own wallet, no yearly fee.

How Bitcoin ETFs Are Taxed

The IRS treats bitcoin as property. Spot bitcoin ETFs are grantor trusts, so you are taxed as if you own your share of the bitcoin — at regular capital gains rates, not the 28% collectibles rate that applies to gold ETFs.

  • Held more than one year: long-term rates of 0%, 15% or 20%.
  • Switching from one bitcoin ETF to another is a taxable sale in a regular account.

Bitcoin ETF Outside the US

Outside the US, a bitcoin ETF is usually a European crypto ETN — open to UK retail investors since the FCA lifted its ban on 8 October 2025 — or a spot bitcoin ETF on the ASX or Cboe Australia. US-listed bitcoin ETFs aren't offered to UK and EU retail investors under PRIIPs rules.

Bitcoin ETF FAQ

What is the best bitcoin ETF?

The cheapest are MSBT (0.14%) and Grayscale's BTC mini trust (0.15%). IBIT (0.25%) is by far the largest, with the most trading volume and options activity.

Why is GBTC so expensive?

GBTC kept the 1.50% fee it charged as a private trust before converting to an ETF in January 2024. The same sponsor's mini trust (BTC) charges 0.15%.

Do bitcoin ETFs hold real bitcoin?

Yes. Spot bitcoin ETFs hold bitcoin with a custodian and publish their holdings. Together they held about 1.29 million BTC on Oct 9, 2026.

Can I buy a bitcoin ETF in my IRA?

Yes, at most brokers. That is the main advantage over buying bitcoin directly, which most IRAs do not allow.

When did bitcoin ETFs start trading?

The SEC approved US spot bitcoin ETFs on January 10, 2024, and they began trading the next day, January 11, 2024.

Can UK investors buy IBIT or other US bitcoin ETFs?

Generally no — US-listed bitcoin ETFs lack the Key Information Document UK and EU rules require. Since 8 October 2025, UK investors can buy LSE-listed crypto ETNs such as the iShares Bitcoin ETP (IB1T) instead.
