Which TSLA ETF Fits Your Goal?

Short-term bullish: TSLL or TSLR. Short-term bearish: TSLS (−1×) or TSLQ (−2×). Income: TSLY. Long-term Tesla exposure with diversification: XLY or ARKK.

  • Leveraged and inverse funds reset daily — see our leveraged ETF guide.
  • Broad funds dilute Tesla's swings with many other holdings.

Is TSLY a Good Income ETF?

TSLY pays weekly, but it holds no Tesla shares and caps its upside. Payouts can include return of capital, so the share price can trend down.

  • Its headline distribution rate can look extremely high, but it is not a yield you can count on; the 30-day SEC yield was 3.17% (Sep 30, 2026).
  • For steadier income, compare dividend ETFs.

TSLA ETF Outside the US

Outside the US, a TSLA ETF usually means a European single-stock leveraged ETP such as Leverage Shares 3x Tesla (TSL3) or GraniteShares 3x Long Tesla (3LTS). There is no plain 1× Tesla fund in Europe, and Australia has no dedicated Tesla ETF.

TSLA ETF FAQ

Is there a Tesla ETF?

There is no plain 1× Tesla ETF, but several funds give Tesla exposure: TSLL, TSLR and TSLT (2× long), TSLS and TSLQ (inverse) and TSLY (option income).

Can you hold TSLL long term?

TSLL targets 2× Tesla's daily move and resets daily. Because Tesla is highly volatile, volatility drag will erode returns during sideways markets, making it best suited for short-term directional trades.

What ETF has the most Tesla?

Among broad funds, XLY holds about 18.21% in Tesla (Oct 8, 2026), and Tesla is ARKK's largest holding at 9.62% (Oct 9, 2026).

Is TSLY safe?

TSLY generates high cash distribution through covered-call option premiums, but caps upside participation during Tesla stock rallies while absorbing downside moves. Its distribution rate fluctuates monthly.

Does TSLL pay dividends?

TSLL is not designed to pay regular dividends; any distributions are occasional.

Is there an ASX-listed Tesla ETF?

No. Australians who want Tesla in a fund usually hold a Nasdaq-100 or global tech ETF, such as Betashares NDQ, where Tesla is one of many holdings.
