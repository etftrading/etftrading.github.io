About ETF Trading

Last updated October 9, 2026

ETF Trading is an independent guide that explains how to buy, sell and hold exchange-traded funds in plain English. We cover investors in the US, UK, Europe and Australia, with the deepest data on US-listed ETFs and their local equivalents.

What we do

We answer the questions people actually ask before they trade ETFs: what they are, what they cost, which platform fits, how taxes work and what can go wrong. Every page leads with the direct answer, followed by the numbers behind it.

Editorial standards

  • Primary sources first. Facts come from regulators (SEC, FINRA, IRS), fund sponsors' prospectuses and broker fee schedules.
  • Dated facts. Fees, availability and rules are checked on the "last updated" date shown on each page and reviewed regularly.
  • No guaranteed returns. Return figures are historical or clearly labeled hypothetical examples.
  • Corrections. If you spot an error, contact us and we will review and correct it promptly.

How we review ETF trading platforms

We compare brokers on the factors that change your results and experience:

  • Costs — commissions on US ETFs, margin rates, transfer and inactivity fees.
  • Access — account types (taxable, IRA, Roth IRA), fractional shares, extended and overnight hours, state availability.
  • Tools — screeners, charting, recurring investments, order types and mobile experience.
  • Safety — SIPC membership, regulatory status and disciplinary history on FINRA BrokerCheck.
  • Fit — which type of investor each platform suits best.

Partner compensation can affect which platforms we feature and where they appear, but it does not change the facts we report. See our advertiser disclosure.
