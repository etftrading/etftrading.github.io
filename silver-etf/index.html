Silver ETF vs. Gold ETF

Silver is both a precious and an industrial metal, so it swings harder than gold. It hit a record $118.45 an ounce in January 2026 (LBMA) and has swung sharply since.

  • The gold/silver ratio — ounces of silver per ounce of gold — has mostly ranged from 40 to 80 since 1978 (Royal Mint).
  • Bigger swings mean bigger gains — and bigger losses.
  • Tax treatment is the same as gold: collectibles rules. Compare funds in our gold ETF trading guide.

How Silver ETFs Are Taxed

SLV and SIVR are taxed as collectibles: long-term gains face a maximum 28% federal rate. PSLV is a Canadian PFIC — with a yearly QEF election (Form 8621), long-term gains can qualify for regular 15–20% rates.

  • Silver miner ETFs such as SIL are taxed like regular stock funds.
  • Holding silver ETFs in an IRA avoids yearly taxes on gains.

Silver ETF Outside the US

A silver ETF outside the US is usually a physically backed silver ETC on the London Stock Exchange, or an ASX-listed product in Australia. US-listed SLV is generally unavailable to UK and EU retail investors under PRIIPs rules.

Silver ETF FAQ

Which silver ETF has the lowest fee?

Among physically backed funds, SIVR at 0.30% a year. SLV charges 0.50% but has far more assets and trading volume.

Is PSLV better than SLV?

PSLV lets large holders redeem for physical silver and can offer better tax treatment with a QEF election, but it can trade at a premium or discount to the value of its silver. SLV tracks its silver value more tightly.

Why is silver more volatile than gold?

Silver's market is much smaller and about half of demand is industrial, so economic news and speculative flows move its price more.

What is a good gold/silver ratio to buy silver?

Traders historically monitor the gold/silver ratio for relative valuation: levels near 80 or above suggest silver is historically inexpensive relative to gold, while levels near 40 indicate relative strength in silver.

How many ounces of silver are in one SLV share?

About 0.903 ounces as of Oct 8, 2026. The amount shrinks slowly as the trust sells silver to pay its 0.50% fee.

Can I buy SLV in the UK?

Not through an ordinary retail account. SLV is a US-domiciled fund without a PRIIPs Key Information Document, so UK and EU brokers can't offer it. A London-listed silver ETC such as Invesco SSLV or iShares SSLN gives the same exposure.
