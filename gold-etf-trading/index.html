How to Start Gold ETF Trading

Open a brokerage account, search a ticker such as GLDM, choose a dollar amount and place a limit order. Gold ETFs trade from 9:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. ET, like any stock.

  • Long holds: pick by fee — IAUM or GLDM.
  • Active trades: pick by liquidity — GLD has the most assets and the deepest options market.
  • Small amounts: fractional shares let you start with a few dollars.

Gold ETFs vs. Physical Gold vs. Gold Miners

Gold ETFs track the metal at the lowest cost. Coins and bars carry dealer premiums and storage costs. Miner ETFs such as GDX move with gold but add company and stock-market risk.

How Gold ETFs Are Taxed

Physically backed gold ETFs are taxed as collectibles: long-term gains face a maximum 28% federal rate instead of 20%. Gains on shares held one year or less are taxed as ordinary income.

  • Applies to GLD, IAU, GLDM, IAUM, SGOL and BAR — trusts that hold metal.
  • If your ordinary income tax rate is below 28%, the lower rate applies.
  • Gold miner ETFs such as GDX are taxed like regular stock funds.
  • Inside an IRA or Roth IRA there is no yearly tax on gains.

Gold ETF Trading Outside the US

Gold ETF trading outside the US usually means physically backed gold ETCs on the London Stock Exchange or Deutsche Börse, or ASX-listed gold products in Australia. US funds like GLD aren't sold to UK and EU retail investors under PRIIPs rules.

Gold ETF Trading FAQ

What is the best gold ETF?

For long-term holding, the lowest-fee funds: IAUM (0.09%) and GLDM (0.10%). For active trading and options, GLD has the most assets and liquidity but charges 0.40% a year.

Is gold ETF trading a good investment?

Gold ETFs serve primarily as a portfolio diversifier and store of value. While gold does not pay yield or dividends, its low correlation with equities can help stabilize a broader portfolio during market volatility.

Do gold ETFs hold real gold?

Yes. Physically backed gold ETFs hold allocated gold bars in vaults, and their sponsors publish the gold held every day.

Why does the gold per share go down over time?

The fund sells a small amount of gold to pay its expense ratio, so each share represents slightly less gold every year. A lower fee means slower shrinkage.

Can I hold gold ETFs in an IRA?

Yes. Most brokers allow gold ETFs in traditional and Roth IRAs, which avoids yearly taxes on gains and is simpler than storing physical gold in a self-directed IRA.

Can I buy GLD in the UK?

Usually not. GLD is a US-domiciled fund without the Key Information Document UK and EU rules require, so retail brokers don't offer it. UK and EU investors use a gold ETC such as iShares Physical Gold (SGLN), which tracks the same gold price.
